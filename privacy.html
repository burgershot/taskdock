Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

TaskDock is a macOS app that lets you view and manage your Google Tasks. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Tasks (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks): your task lists and tasks, so they can be displayed, created, edited, completed, moved, and deleted at your request.
  • Basic account info (scopes openid and email): your email address, shown in the app's settings so you can see which account is signed in.

How data is used and stored

  • TaskDock runs only on your Mac. It communicates directly with Google's APIs over HTTPS. There is no TaskDock server, and no data is sent to the developer or any third party.
  • Your tasks are kept in memory while the app is running and are fetched from Google again when needed. They are not stored on disk by TaskDock.
  • The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored locally on your Mac in a file only your user account can read. App preferences (such as accent color) are stored in standard macOS preferences.
  • TaskDock contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • Data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising, and is never used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

TaskDock's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Sign out in TaskDock (Settings → Account) to delete the stored tokens and revoke the app's access.
  • You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the app and the folder ~/Library/Application Support/TaskDock removes everything it stored locally.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
